# PakEco AI — 2–3 Minute Hackathon Demo

## 1. Introduction — 20 sec

"Hello, this is PakEco AI, an AI-powered environmental pollution monitoring and city-comparison platform for Pakistan."

## 2. Architecture — 20 sec

"Our application has a shared Python backend, a Gradio AI frontend and a Streamlit analytics dashboard. The backend connects to Open-Meteo and Gemini."

## 3. Gradio — 45 sec

"Here is the Gradio interface. I select Karachi and retrieve pollution information. I can see US AQI, PM2.5, PM10 and weather information."

"Now I compare multiple Pakistani cities using the comparison tab."

## 4. Gemini — 30 sec

"I can ask the Gemini Environmental Assistant: Why is PM2.5 important? The assistant receives the dashboard context and explains it in simple language."

## 5. Streamlit — 35 sec

"The Streamlit dashboard provides the same backend data with a richer analytics layout, including metrics, trends, tables and AQI comparison charts."

## 6. Responsible AI — 20 sec

"A key design decision is transparency: these air-quality values are model-based data and are not presented as official ground-station measurements."

## 7. Closing — 10 sec

"PakEco AI shows how environmental data, AI and accessible web interfaces can make pollution information easier to explore across Pakistan."
